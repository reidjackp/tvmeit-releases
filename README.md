# TVmeit

TVmeit is an app for watching your own IPTV subscription on **Android TV, Google TV and Fire TV Stick**. It's a player: it has no channels, films or shows of its own. You sign in with the login your provider gives you, and TVmeit turns what they send into something easy to watch.

**Downloader code: 7537926**

## What it does

- **Signs in with your provider's Xtream login or playlist link.** Your login is stored encrypted on your TV and only ever sent to your provider.
- **Turns a huge, messy list into a short, tidy one.** Providers often send tens of thousands of entries. TVmeit keeps your country's channels in clear groups (Entertainment, Sport, News, Films, Kids and so on), with the most-watched channels first.
- **Shows one channel, not ten copies of it.** When a provider lists the same channel many times, TVmeit merges them into one and keeps the rest as backups.
- **Lists pay-per-view events only while they're on or coming up,** at the right UK time.
- **TV guide, search, favourites, films and series,** with Continue watching and next-episode.

## Why it works so well

- **It picks the copy that really plays best.** Labels like "4K" or "FHD" are often wrong, so TVmeit measures each copy's actual picture, frame rate and how quickly it starts, and plays the best one.
- **It moves on by itself.** If a copy freezes, keeps buffering or drops, TVmeit switches to the next one without you doing anything.
- **It reads event times properly.** Providers write times in each feed's own time zone (New York, Sydney, GMT). TVmeit converts them all to UK time, clock changes included.
- **It tells you what's wrong.** If channels won't load, it says whether it's the connection, your VPN or the provider.
- **It's small and light.** 2.5 MB, and built to run on 1 GB Fire TV Sticks.
- **No ads, no tracking, no account with us.** TVmeit doesn't collect anything about you or what you watch.

## Install on a Fire TV Stick

1. Install the free **Downloader** app from the Amazon Appstore.
2. Go to Settings > My Fire TV > Developer options > Install unknown apps, and turn it on for Downloader. (If you can't see Developer options, open My Fire TV > About and click the device name 7 times.)
3. In Downloader, type the code **7537926** and press Go.
4. Install it, open TVmeit and sign in with your provider's login.

**On Android TV or Google TV:** TVmeit isn't on the Play Store, but Downloader is. Install **Downloader by AFTVnews** from the Play Store, allow it to install apps (Settings > Apps > Security & restrictions > Unknown sources), then follow steps 3 and 4.

**Updating:** enter the same code again. Your sign-in and favourites stay.
